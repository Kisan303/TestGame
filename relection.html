You are carrying a prototype light-field scanner inside an abandoned laboratory.

FIRST CONTACT

Before entering the laboratory, learn how the LUMEN device works.

🧭 Motion Allow motion access on iPhone/iPad. Hold the phone normally. Tilt it gently to aim. On PC use WASD.
🎤 Microphone Allow microphone access. Speak, clap or make noise. The Hollow reacts to sound.
📷 Camera SCAN requests your rear camera. Camera mode is optional.
🔦 PULSE Consumes 18 energy. It pushes The Hollow away.
🔬 OBSERVER Switches to a laboratory overview. Inspect light rays and shadows.
⏸ PAUSE Stops the simulation completely. SAVE stores your progress locally.

This game does not require a server for sensors. Camera and microphone permissions are requested only when you activate those features.
